About {Brand}

{Brand} gives education and tools, not personal advice: free calculators and plain-English guides. We don't manage money or offer bank or investment accounts, loans, investments or other financial services.

What we build and why

{Brand} makes free calculators and plain-English guides about money. They show how things like compound interest work. Each one gives the formula and a worked example, so you can follow every step.

They are educational. They explain how the numbers work. They don't tell anyone what to do with their own money.

A stepwell is a well with steps down to water saved for dry seasons. Here you can learn how savings grow, one step at a time, and check every number yourself.

How we check the numbers

Every formula is written on the page that uses it. Before a page goes live, its worked examples and test figures are recomputed by a separate reviewer that did not build it, and the results are kept in our review records. Mistakes can still get through. If you find one, tell us (see below).
