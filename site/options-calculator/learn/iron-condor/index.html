Iron condor

What it is

A put and a call are sold at inner strikes; a put below and a call above are bought.

Worked example

Options can lose all the money paid for them. Selling an option can lose far more than the money received for it, and some positions have no limit on the loss. Nothing here is advice to buy, sell or use any option.

For education only, not financial advice.

Every price, strike and premium here is a made-up example until you type your own. A profit or loss here is arithmetic on the numbers shown. It does not show what any real option has done or would do.

Example legs
SideTypeStrikePremium per share
Bought (long)Put$40.00$0.50
Sold (short)Put$45.00$1.50
Sold (short)Call$55.00$1.25
Bought (long)Call$60.00$0.40

Example: $40.00 put bought ($0.50), $45.00 put sold ($1.50), $55.00 call sold ($1.25), $60.00 call bought ($0.40): credit $1.85; most made $185.00 from $45.00 to $55.00; most lost $315.00; break-evens $43.15 and $56.85 per share. Bought form (the reverse): the signs flip.

Maximum profit (example)
$185.00, for any price from $45.00 to $55.00
Maximum loss (example)
$315.00
Break-even prices (example)
$43.15 and $56.85 per share
Net credit received (example)
$1.85 per share

For education only, not financial advice.

What can go wrong

The most lost is more than the credit received.

About this tool. The option modeler works out what a position typed by you would pay at expiry. It uses only the numbers you type and the simplifications listed under "What can go wrong".

It does not know what any option costs, whether a price will rise or fall, or which positions are available in any market. The calculation runs in your browser; the numbers you type are not stored, saved, logged or sent anywhere by this tool.

The results are illustrations of arithmetic. They do not show what any real option has done or would do. Options can lose all the money paid for them, and selling an option can lose far more than the money received for it.

A result is arithmetic on the numbers typed and may not match a real position. We do not give financial advice, and nothing on this page is advice to buy, sell or use any option. This tool is also not investment advice, tax advice or legal advice.
