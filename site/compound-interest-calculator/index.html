How this calculator works

It uses a common convention for savings accounts. Inside each compounding period your money earns simple interest month by month, and at the end of the period that interest is added to your balance and starts earning interest of its own. Your bank may work interest out differently, for example daily on your balance, so its figures can differ slightly from these. With daily compounding it uses the exact daily growth for each month. Contributions at the start of a month earn interest that month; contributions at the end start earning the next month. Every year's figure is worked out directly from the formula, so no rounding builds up. The multiple under each end figure is the final balance divided by everything put in (the starting amount plus every contribution), and the percent beside Compounding adds is what compounding adds divided by the simple balance; both are rounded to one decimal place.Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to us or stored. If you use Copy link, your numbers are written into that link, so anyone you share it with can see them.
